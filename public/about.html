Life Path — Pythagorean Ledger

Life Path is a free Pythagorean numerology calculator that uses the classical tripartite reduction method to compute Life Path and Expression numbers. The calculation engine is open-source and mathematically auditable.

  • Method: Independent reduction of month, day, and year before final summation
  • Master Numbers: 11, 22, and 33 are preserved without further reduction
  • Privacy: All free calculations happen entirely in your browser — no data leaves your device
  • Source: View the source code and math audit on GitHub
