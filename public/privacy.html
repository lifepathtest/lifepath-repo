Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Life Path (life-path.icu) is operated by APEX Business Systems Ltd., Edmonton, AB, Canada. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What We Collect

Calculator Usage (No Account Required)

When you use the free Life Path calculator, your birth date and name are processed entirely in your browser. No personal data is sent to our servers for the free calculation, and your birth date and name are never included in our usage analytics. If you click a purchase button, your birth date is passed to Stripe as described in section 2.

Usage Analytics Events

We record a small set of usage events (e.g., "page viewed," "result shown," "checkout button clicked") to measure how the site performs. Each event stores only:

  • Event name (e.g., quiz_viewed, free_result_shown, checkout_redirect)
  • Timestamp
  • A random per-visit identifier generated in your browser (not linked to your identity)
  • Your country, as reported by Cloudflare
  • Whether the visit looked automated (bot) or human — your browser's user-agent is checked for this but not stored
  • Where relevant: the pricing tier you clicked, your calculated Life Path number, and whether you entered a name (yes/no only)

These events are stored in a Supabase database. They do not include your IP address, name, birth date, or email.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, collects aggregated, privacy-first analytics (page views, country, referrer). Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track individual users. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

2. Payments (Stripe)

If you purchase a report, payment is processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not see or store your credit card number. Stripe collects your email address and payment details under their own privacy policy. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

When you click a purchase button, your calculated Life Path number and birth date are added to the Stripe checkout link as a reference (for example, lp11_19900512) so you don't have to retype your birth date. Your name is not included.

After purchase, we receive a confirmation from Stripe containing your email, the tier purchased, that checkout reference, and any details you enter at checkout (such as your full birth name). We use these solely to fulfill your report.

3. Cookies

Life Path does not set any first-party cookies. No tracking cookies, no session cookies, no advertising cookies. Cloudflare may set a minimal security cookie (__cf_bm) for bot detection purposes. The random per-visit identifier described above is kept in your browser's session storage (not a cookie) and is removed when you close the tab.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except:

  • Stripe — for payment processing (as described above)
  • Cloudflare — for hosting, CDN, and security
  • Supabase — for usage event storage (no IP address, name, birth date, or email)

5. Data Retention

Usage analytics events are retained for up to 12 months. Stripe transaction records are retained as required by applicable financial regulations.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data associated with your email address by contacting us. Since our calculator is anonymous and requires no account, there is typically no personal data to delete from the calculator itself.

7. Children's Privacy

Life Path is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
